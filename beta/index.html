Thanks for testing Kaimono

Beta guide · about 5 minutes to read

What it does: snap a price tag while shopping (in Japan or anywhere) and it tells you what the same thing costs and resells for back home, with the profit after your costs.

1. Install it

  • iPhone: open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open it from the new icon from then on.
  • Android: open the app in Chrome and tap Install when it offers (or menu → Install app).
  • Allow the camera when asked. Location is optional: it only remembers which shop you scanned in.

2. Pick your home country

Settings → Home country: where you'd buy it otherwise, or resell it. Prices, money and links follow it.

3. What to try

  • Price tags in shops. Electronics, games, cameras, figures, watches, anything you'd consider reselling. The more different shops the better (Hard Off, Book Off, 2nd Street, Bic Camera, Don Quijote…).
  • A tag with a barcode. Get the barcode in the photo too; it should say Barcode match.
  • Second-hand items with a grade (A, B, C, ジャンク): switch to Used first.
  • Clothes: pick Clothes, photograph the whole piece, then its labels.
  • A whole shelf: pick Shelf to price everything at once.
  • Something you buy: tap Bought?, and when you sell it, type what it sold for. That's how we learn whether the profit figures are honest.

4. Tell us when it's wrong

  • Under every result: Right model and prices? Tap Yes, or Not quite and pick what was off (wrong model, a price misread, new or used price off, CeX off). A word on what it should be helps a lot. This is the most useful thing you can do.
  • Tap any price to see the listings behind it. If they're for a different model, that's a Not quite.
  • Anything else (confusing, slow, missing, broken): Settings → Send feedback.
  • Crashes and failed scans report themselves; you don't need to do anything.

5. Scans and paying

You get free scans each month (Home shows how many are left), and a product someone scanned by barcode in the last week is free again. If you've been given a tester code, use it on the payment page under Add promotion code to get a plan without paying.

Good to know

  • Prices come from AI and web search. They're estimates and can be wrong: that's what the beta is for.
  • Your scans and photos stay on your phone. See the privacy policy.
  • Removing the app from your home screen deletes its data. Settings → Back up first if you reinstall.
